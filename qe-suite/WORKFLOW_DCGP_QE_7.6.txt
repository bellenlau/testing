Quantum ESPRESSO 7.6 on Leonardo DCGP: minimal workflow

Run from a DCGP compute allocation:

cd /leonardo_scratch/large/userinternal/lbellen1/vscode/testing/qe-suite
./launch.sh --version 7.6 --stack-file stacks/dcgp.env

The stack profile loads the CPU compiler, MPI and MKL modules declared in
stacks/dcgp.env. It reuses a matching QE 7.6 installation when pw.x is
available; otherwise it clones qe-7.6 and builds it with CMake-generated
Makefiles under testing/work.

To prepare and submit the complete QE test suite:

./launch.sh --version 7.6 --stack-file stacks/dcgp.env --submit

Add --wait to wait for the Slurm job and print the result of each category.
