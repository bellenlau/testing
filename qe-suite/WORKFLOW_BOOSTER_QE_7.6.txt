Quantum ESPRESSO 7.6 on Leonardo Booster: minimal workflow

Run from a Booster compute allocation:

cd /leonardo_scratch/large/userinternal/lbellen1/vscode/testing/qe-suite
./launch.sh --version 7.6 --stack-file stacks/booster.env

The stack profile loads CMake, Intel oneAPI MKL and the supplied
NVHPC/HPC-X CUDA 12.9 module. It reuses a matching QE 7.6 installation when
pw.x is available; otherwise it clones qe-7.6 and builds it with CMake under
testing/work. CMake generates Unix Makefiles and the compilation is run with
make -j 8.
